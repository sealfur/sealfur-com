    As often happens, the dedication to notes (and, [this time, photos](https://flic.kr/s/aHBqjCUJ3R)) waned in proportion to bellies getting fuller. Starting at Little Prince, once again, made me wonder why I don’t go there more often for the delicious food, wine and atmosphere. But living on the other side of St Kilda is the easy answer there. It’s difficult to walk past Cicciolina to spend whatever indulgence budget I may have.
    
    **Little Prince**’s salami skewers were warm, fatty, salty bites of deliciousness: the kind of joy that is probably discouraged by <i>Leviticus</i> and my gastroenterologist. Zucchini chips were in a lovely, light tempura, but were overall deemed too oily and salty (so there *is* such a thing).

    **Akwaaba** described their [chicken giblets](https://flic.kr/p/2seUKQC) as “tossed in a green capsicum sauce, served on a bed of rocket”. They were just kind of generically spicy and a little too tough to enjoy that many of them. Could not see any rocket.

    The highlight at **Buba** was the [Moroccan carrots that came with the lamb kebab](https://flic.kr/p/2seVwqb). Unfortunately my kebab was undercooked, but what I ate of it was tasty. They also have a really delicious tahini, which I could eat on everything.

    **Bistro Voliere**’s b&#339;uf bourguignon was perfect! All it missed was a bit of baguette to soak up all the gravy (which I did drink from the plate).

    Biggest surprise were the offerings from **Tacqueria**. These were not listed on their menu, so they might be using the event to try new offerings. Both tacos were incredibly delicious. The suadero was a beef cut of brisket that was mostly flavoured by its own slow-cook process. The salsa tipped it over into one of those street food secrets you want to watch others eat, to get some of that secondary, reflected joy.

    This was all part of [“Around the World in One Day: Long Lunch Edition”](https://fitzroystreetstkilda.com.au/event/around-the-world-in-one-day/#offers) in Fitzroy Street, St&nbsp;Kilda.
